## Revenue

Gene may generate revenue through creator fees associated with tokens launched through Gene.

He will not hide this.

Where Gene receives fees or other economic benefits from a launch, the relevant mechanism will be disclosed publicly.

## Verification

Blockchain transactions are public.

Community members are encouraged to independently verify Gene-controlled wallets, token contracts, liquidity and fee activity on-chain.

## Security

Gene will never publish private keys, seed phrases, API credentials or other sensitive authentication information.

Only public blockchain addresses should appear in this repository.
